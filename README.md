🏫 CAMPUS BRIDGE

AI-Powered Student Learning & Career Development Platform

Campus Bridge is a unified web and mobile platform designed to help students learn, manage academics, build technical and soft skills, prepare for careers, create professional resumes, practice interviews, collaborate with other students, and discover placement and internship opportunities.

The platform combines AI-powered learning assistance, career guidance, resume building, mock interviews, academic tracking, community collaboration, and career-readiness analytics into one student-focused ecosystem.

🎯 19. Project Objective

The primary objective of Campus Bridge is to create a single platform where students can manage their academic learning and career development without depending on multiple disconnected applications.

The platform aims to:

- Improve student learning
- Provide AI-powered academic assistance
- Organize study materials
- Track academic progress
- Build professional resumes
- Provide interview practice
- Identify skill gaps
- Recommend career paths
- Connect students with opportunities
- Support student collaboration
- Improve overall career readiness
